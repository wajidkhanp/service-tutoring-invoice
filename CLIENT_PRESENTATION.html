The Challenge

Manual invoicing wastes time

Tutoring centers managing 20+ students face recurring friction every billing cycle. Here's what that looks like:

📋

Repetitive manual work

Creating individual invoices by hand for every student, every month — prone to errors and inconsistency.

📧

Email bottleneck

Attaching PDFs, addressing emails to each parent, and sending one by one takes hours per cycle.

🗂️

No central history

Invoices scattered across folders and inboxes make it hard to answer "was this student billed in March?"

🔢

Rate management overhead

Different rates per student require manual lookup and calculation for every invoice, every time.

Security & Privacy

Secure by design

Built for handling sensitive billing data — student names, parent emails, and financial records.

🔐

Google OAuth — no passwords stored

Login is handled entirely by Google. No passwords are stored anywhere in the system.

🎯

Allowlist access — only authorized accounts

Only explicitly approved Google accounts can log in. No open registration.

🚫

PDFs never stored on disk

Invoice PDFs are generated in memory and streamed directly to the browser or email. Nothing is written to the server.

🔒

HTTPS everywhere

All traffic is encrypted via SSL/TLS. Railway provisions and renews the certificate automatically.

🏠

Private deployment — no public data exposure

Student and invoice data is stored in private volumes on Railway — not accessible to the public.
